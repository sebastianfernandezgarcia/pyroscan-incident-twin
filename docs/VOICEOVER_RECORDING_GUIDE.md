# PyroScan challenge film — voiceover recording guide

The final visual master is **2:54 (174 seconds), 1920×1080, 30 fps**. The approved continuous recording is split at natural pauses into ten aligned takes so the voice is never time-stretched.

## Recording setup

- Quiet, soft room; phone or microphone 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- Dry voice only: no music, reverb, compression, or AI cleanup.
- Preferred format: mono WAV or AIFF, 48 kHz. M4A is also fine.
- Leave about half a second of room tone before and after each line.
- Name the files `01.wav` through `10.wav` and place them in `videos/pyroscan-challenge-film/voiceover/takes/`.
- Read the sentence naturally at roughly 112–120 spoken words per minute, then let the remaining slot breathe. Do not rush to fill silence.

## Pronunciation

- **PyroScan:** “PY-ro-scan.”
- **WebMCP:** “Web M-C-P.”
- **ChatGPT:** “Chat G-P-T.”
- **La Palma / El Paso:** natural Spanish pronunciation.
- **LP-3:** “L-P-three.”

## Timed script

### 01 · 00:00–00:10 · Before the fire

> Before a wildfire becomes a live emergency, teams need somewhere to rehearse the decisions that come first.

Open slowly. Stress **before** and **rehearse**.

### 02 · 00:10–00:24 · Context breaks

> Local knowledge lives in people. Spatial evidence lives on maps. And an AI assistant usually reasons somewhere else. Every handoff loses context.

Use three measured statements, then a firmer conclusion.

### 03 · 00:24–00:40 · Product reveal

> PyroScan Incident Twin turns the browser into one shared rehearsal table, designed around La Palma's real geography and constrained access.

Warm confidence; let **one shared rehearsal table** land.

### 04 · 00:40–00:58 · The protocol

> WebMCP lets the page expose its real verbs directly to ChatGPT: read, inspect, annotate, simulate, compare, and stage. No separate server. No brittle click script.

Give every verb a tiny beat. Keep the last two sentences crisp.

### 05 · 00:58–01:20 · Local knowledge

> I ask at the level of intent. ChatGPT focuses El Paso and adds a blocked-road exercise note for the LP-3 checkpoint. That local constraint becomes visible to me—and available to every later tool call.

Sound like a live demo, not an advertisement. Stress **visible** and **every later tool call**.

### 06 · 01:20–01:40 · Bounded what-if

> Next, a sixty-minute northeast wind shift redraws the deterministic attention area on the same map. This is not a forecast; it is a bounded what-if that a person and an agent can inspect together.

Pause clearly before **This is not a forecast**.

### 07 · 01:40–01:58 · Compare trade-offs

> PyroScan compares two reversible response options against setup time, coverage, resource use, and the new access constraint. The ranking lives on screen—not buried in a chat transcript.

Analytical and matter-of-fact. Land **on screen**.

### 08 · 01:58–02:20 · State lineage

> Before staging, ChatGPT reads the exact board version. If the evidence changed, a stale plan is rejected. The new draft carries the LP-3 constraint into its access action, proving that human knowledge changed the downstream artifact.

Slightly slower; this is the technical credibility beat.

### 09 · 02:20–02:34 · Human gate

> The agent can explore. It cannot approve. Final approval is deliberately absent from the WebMCP tools and remains with the exercise director.

Short, deliberate sentences. Emphasize **cannot approve** and **exercise director**.

### 10 · 02:34–02:54 · Close

> Synthetic. Deterministic. Browser-only. Open source. No live feed, dispatch, or hidden backend. The ambition: rehearse trade-offs before a crisis makes them irreversible. PyroScan. Wildfire readiness, rehearsed together.

Build from factual to hopeful. Pause before **PyroScan**. Finish warmly, not loudly.

## Final mix target

After recording, the assembly script places the ten takes at their exact cue points, filters rumble, normalizes the combined narration to approximately **−16 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP**, and muxes it into the approved visual master. The provisional subtitle file is `videos/pyroscan-challenge-film/voiceover/PyroScan_VO_EN.srt`; its timings should be checked once the real voice is available.
