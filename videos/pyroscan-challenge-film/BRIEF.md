## Intent

Create a cinematic but evidence-led product film for the OpenAI WebMCP Challenge. Sell the preventive idea, then prove it with the real PyroScan interface: one shared La Palma incident twin where a person adds local context, an agent explores bounded what-ifs through WebMCP, and the person retains the final decision. The tone is urgent, precise, hopeful and technically credible—not disaster spectacle.

## Assets

- https://sebastianfernandezgarcia.github.io/pyroscan-incident-twin/ — real product interface and WebMCP experience.
- docs/assets/devpost-hero-16x9.jpg — current product hero capture.
- src/domain/laPalmaGeometry.ts — recognizable La Palma outline for motion-design explainers.
- src/webmcp/registerIncidentTools.ts — the six real tool contracts and implementation evidence.

## Customizations

- Feature real captured screens as the proof layer; use invented motion graphics only to explain the human↔WebMCP↔shared-board relationship.
- Build a timed English narration script and caption track so Sebastián can record the final voiceover separately.
- Include a clear full journey: inspect El Paso, add a blocked-road note, simulate a 60-minute northeast shift, compare responses, and stage a reversible plan.
- Show that stale board versions are rejected and final approval is deliberately human-only.
- Create a 3:2 thumbnail/gallery system from the same visual language.

## Notes

- Synthetic exercise only. Never imply a live fire, forecast, dispatch capability, government deployment, real-world prevention outcome, or live data feed.
- No unauthorized trademarks or copyrighted music. Use only original interface visuals, attributed OpenStreetMap-derived coastline, and original/procedural audio if any.
- Target 2:40 and never exceed 2:55 after the final human voiceover.
- The final film must visibly demonstrate the working WebMCP product, not only present motion graphics.
- Autonomous design choice: the Broadside preset was remixed onto PyroScan's dark, acid-green visual system because its declarative typography adds urgency without competing with the product screens.
