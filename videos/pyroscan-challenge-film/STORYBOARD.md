## Video direction

PyroScan's dark surface is the constant world: `frame.md` supplies the near-black canvas, warm white type and acid-green voltage. Opening and closing frames use the declarative Broadside register; proof frames place real 3:2 application captures inside restrained floating surfaces. Motion is smooth and forensic: sequential reveals land on spoken cues, one clear focal point per beat, velocity-matched internal cuts, and held reads after each result. The real interface carries at least half of the total runtime. Captions will occupy the bottom 17%; all essential content stays in the top 83%. Stillness is allocated to the safety claim and final lockup. No bounce, screensaver drift, disaster spectacle, generic AI particles, fake forecasts, infinite loops or front-loaded slide animation.
